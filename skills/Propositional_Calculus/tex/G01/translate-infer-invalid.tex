\[
\begin{array}{rll}
\text{p1}. & H \rightarrow (E_1\vee E_2) & \equiv \neg H\vee E_1\vee E_2 \\
\text{p2}. & \neg C \rightarrow (\neg E_1\wedge\neg E_2) &
  \equiv C\vee(\neg E_1\wedge\neg E_2) \\
\text{neg-c}. & \neg(\neg H\rightarrow(E_1\wedge E_2)) &
  \equiv \neg H\wedge(\neg E_1\vee\neg E_2)
\end{array}
\]

\begin{center}
\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{%
\begin{forest}
  truth tree,
  for tree={s sep=3.5em}
  [{ \textit{from neg-c} \\ $\neg H$ }
    [{ $\neg E_1$ }
      [{ \textit{Apply p1} \\ $\neg H$ }
        [{ \textit{Apply p2} \\ $C$ }
          [{ $\mathbf{\circ}$ \\ \textit{Open counterexample} \\ $C,\neg H,\neg E_1$ }]
        ]
        [{ \textit{Apply p2} \\ $\neg E_1\wedge\neg E_2$ }
          [{ $\neg E_1$ \\ $\neg E_2$ \\ $\mathbf{\circ}$ \\ \textit{Open} }]
        ]
      ]
      [{ \textit{Apply p1} \\ $E_1$ }
        [{ $\mathbf{X}$ \\ \textit{Contradiction} \\ $E_1,\neg E_1$ }]
      ]
      [{ \textit{Apply p1} \\ $E_2$ \\ \textit{not expanded} }]
    ]
    [{ $\neg E_2$ \\ \textit{not expanded} }]
  ]
\end{forest}
}

Once the open branch $C,\neg H,\neg E_1$ is found, the remaining branches
need not be expanded: one counterexample is enough to show invalidity.
\end{center}
